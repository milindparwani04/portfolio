## Championship escalation — exactly one engine event

Trigger once when **two distinct participating champion Pokémon have fainted**, not at half HP, two player faints, or repeated fainting of the same revived member. Define the event at a safe end-of-resolution boundary before replacement/player requests are exposed. Handle simultaneous KOs, residual damage, forced switches and a terminal outcome explicitly. Recommended: if the player has no usable Pokémon, resolve loss rather than resurrecting a concluded match. Never revive a species left outside the participating four.

Champion dialogue, fiery arena lighting, championship stadium/crowd reveal and cheering build for **38 seconds**, then revival lands on the musical drop and the battle resumes. Fire is visual unless separately specified; do not secretly add weather or stat boosts.

Recommended interpretation for “revives their Pokémon”: revive the two fainted participating champion members once each at **50% maximum HP**, rounded with an engine-consistent minimum of 1, without resetting PP, item consumption or the entire team. Confirm this versus reviving only one in Phase 0. Specify status/volatile reset, active-slot restoration and side counters. Use a dedicated simulator-supported encounter rule, not DOM HP edits or blindly mutating `fainted`; inspect how Showdown handles revival and recomputes requests/win conditions. Record faint/revive events and the once-only flag in replay state. No additional action may be queued while paused; guard the resume acknowledgement against duplicates and stale battles.

Timeline recommendation: 0–10s respectful dialogue; 10–26s arena expands/illuminates and crowd emerges; 26–36s final line and revival preparation; 36–38s final effect; exactly 38s apply/commit revival and unlock a fresh valid battle request.
