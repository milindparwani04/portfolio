Privacy

Last updated 30 September 2026

What this site shows

milindparwani.com is a personal portfolio. Some panels show live data about me, the site owner, pulled from services I have connected my own accounts to:

  • Spotify — what I'm listening to, my public playlists, and my top tracks and artists this month.
  • Google Health — my most recent heart-rate reading from my Fitbit.

Only my own accounts are connected. The site never asks visitors to sign in to Spotify, Google or anything else, and it can't read visitors' data from those services.

What is stored

  • Access tokens for my own Spotify and Google Health accounts, stored server-side and never sent to the browser.
  • A log of my own Spotify plays, used to count this month's top tracks and artists.
  • For heart rate, only the latest reading is fetched and shown; no heart-rate history is stored.

Health data is used only to show that single reading on this site. It is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train any model.

Visitors

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. It is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes standard request data (such as IP address) to serve and protect the site under Cloudflare's privacy policy. The browser tools on the site run locally; files you use with them are not uploaded.
